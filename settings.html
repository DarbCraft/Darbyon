Daily Refill

Each morning (Perth time) every player gets some AP back, and every few days some HP. Nobody goes above their class's maximum: Warrior 10 HP and 15 AP, Healer 8 HP and 20 AP, Mage 6 HP and 30 AP. It counts every day, weekends too.

Power Pack

Adds 23 Classcraft-style powers: Protect, Heal, Revive, Mana Transfer, Time Warp and more, each for one class and unlocked at a level. Powers you already have with the same name are left alone.
